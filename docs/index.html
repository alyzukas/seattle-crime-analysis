Crime at Three Scales

DATA 5310 Data Visualization, Seattle University, Fall 2026. Final project.

Our group looks at crime from 2010 to 2024 at three scales: one city, one country, and the world. Each section below is one group member's notebook.

  • Seattle

    Crime reported to the Seattle Police Department.

  • United States

    Crime across the United States.

  • World

    The United States compared with other countries, using United Nations and World Bank data.
